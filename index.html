SaaS · PWA · Live at a college

      
Adhyora

      
A college management platform with five role-based portals: Principal, Teacher (with extra HOD options), Staff, Accountant and Student. It covers realtime attendance, timetables, chat, assignments with reminders and teacher dashboards. It supports both NEP and traditional syllabus structures. One college uses it today and more are evaluating it.

      
  • Principals watch attendance live, including which teachers have not marked yet
  • Configurable attendance logic: session, hour or subject-based calculation
  • College-scoped Firestore security rules, push notifications, Razorpay payments, installable PWA
